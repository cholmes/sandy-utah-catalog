# AGENTS.md — Public Utilities

17 collections. Most GeoParquet here is **EPSG:3566** (NAD83(HARN) / Utah Central, US survey feet), but not all of it: `lead-service-lines` is EPSG:2850 (metres). Check `proj:epsg` on the collection before computing any length or area. Each collection's own `AGENTS.md` carries its schema, its coded-value lists and a query that runs.
